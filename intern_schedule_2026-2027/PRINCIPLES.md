# Principles of Scheduling Interns at LSH — Comprehensive Rules

*Source of truth: `Principles_of_scheduling_interns_at_LSH` (5/14/25). Where an
older document conflicts, this governs. These rules are encoded in
`generator/gen.py` and checked by `generator/audit.py`.*

## Interns

Four interns rotate at any time: **LSH1, LSH2** (both from LSH), **one BMC-South**
(formerly Good Samaritan) and **one Lahey**.

- BMC-South rotates in **2- or 4-week** blocks. Blocks **1, 2, 5, 8, 9, 12** are
  covered by **Anesthesia interns from BMC Brighton** (formerly St. Elizabeth's);
  blocks **3, 4, 6, 7, 10, 11** by BMC-South interns.

## Shifts

- **Long Call (LC):** Sun–Thu 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM; **Fri 7:00 AM – 7:30 PM**;
  Saturday = the 24-hour shift. (Column C.)
- **Short Call (SC):** 7:00 AM – 3:30 PM. Usually 2 interns on weekdays, 1 on
  weekends. (Column D.)
- **Night Float (NF):** Sun–Thu 6:00 PM – 8:00 AM; **Fri 7:30 PM – 9:30 AM**
  (rounds Saturday morning). No night float on Saturday (covered by the 24h).
- **Saturday 24h:** one intern, 7 AM Sat – 7 AM Sun.

## The integrated Q4 "march" (core rotation)

The four slots cycle in the order **[LSH1, Lahey, LSH2, BMC]**. Each week one slot
is night float; the night-float slot advances by one each week. Within that:

1. **One Long Call per day**, and **every intern is on Long Call every 4th day
   (Q4)** — each weekday's long-call intern differs from the previous day's.
2. **The Friday Long-Call intern becomes the next week's Night Float** (starts
   Sunday night).
3. **Night Float runs consecutively Sun→Fri**, the **same person the entire
   block** (4–7 nights). After the last (Friday) night, that intern **returns to
   Monday Long Call** and resumes the Q4 march.
4. **The Saturday 24h is the week's "middle" intern** (two slots ahead of the
   night float). Over four weeks each slot takes exactly one Saturday.

## Night Float details

- Starts Sunday → continues through Friday night (last night Friday).
- If it starts **Monday** (because of where the 1st of the month falls), it runs
  **Monday → Friday**.
- **Month/rotation boundaries:** a departing intern may do a few nights and the
  arriving intern continues the block ("a new intern starts night float when the
  month ends"). Blocks are 4–7 consecutive nights per hospital.
- No intern is on daytime (LC/SC) **and** night float the same day.

## Saturday 24h details

- Only that one intern works Saturday — **no short call, no night float** that day.
- **Each hospital (LSH, Lahey, BMC-South) gets at least one Saturday per month**;
  **each intern does one 24h Saturday per 4-week block, and no other 24h that
  block.**
- Whoever does the Saturday 24h **cannot be night float the next day (Sunday)** —
  they are off Sunday (return Monday; no clinical work for ≥14 hours / that
  Sunday after a ≥24h shift).

## Days off

- **Everyone gets at least one day off per week** (a 24h period their name is not
  on the schedule).
- The preferred day off is **Thursday**, and **only one intern is off at a time**.
  If a day off must fall on a weekday, it is Thursday.
- Everyone not on night float, long call, or off is written under **Short Call**
  (so weekdays show one long call + the rest short call, minus the single Thursday
  off).

## Excel formatting

- Header: Long Call / Short Call under **"Intern – Day"**; night float under
  **"Intern – Night Float."**
- Columns: **A Day, B Date, C Long Call, D Short Call, E Night Float / Sat 24h.**
  (The source document also describes an E+F merged night-float column; the
  finalized September sheet uses the single-column-E layout, which is what these
  files match.)
- **Saturday and Sunday rows are bold; all assignments are bold.**
- **Sunday has no one under Short Call.**

## Duty hours (ACGME)

- ≤ 80 clinical hours/week averaged over 4 weeks; no duty period > 28h (the 24h
  Saturday is the longest); ≥ 1 day off/week.
