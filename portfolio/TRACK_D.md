## What is actually being sold

Not "quant researcher." The deliverable is narrower, more current, and harder to find:

- **Auditing a quantitative claim before capital or a decision goes behind it** — a backtest, a model,
  an internal result that looks too good.
- **Designing pre-registered evaluations** for a team that keeps producing results that don't replicate.
- **Building verification harnesses around AI-generated analysis** — negative controls, blind
  replication, adversarial review.

That third one is the differentiated offer, and the portfolio is unusually good evidence for it,
because the four documented cases are all instances of the apparatus catching **the author's own
error** before it became a result:

| case | what it caught |
|---|---|
| 01 | a frozen reading that would have reported **8,194 arbitrages that did not exist** (98.9% dissolved at the close price) |
| 02 | an **outcome-conditioned price rule** that, read literally, made every trade a winner — refused by two blind builds |
| 03 | **three KILL clauses that could not fire**, each for a different structural reason, found and corrected |
| 04 | a flawless signal with **8 tradeable instances out of 2,850** — the market had already moved eight hours earlier |

Nobody advertises their own caught errors. That is exactly why it is credible, and it is the asset
this repository actually contains.

## The honest framing, which is also the stronger pitch

The page's footer says it plainly: **the studies were designed, directed and adjudicated by you, and
executed by AI agents** under blind builds, frozen specs, an adversarial verifier and negative
controls. Most of the defects listed were defects in the *specifications*, caught because the

Presenting AI-executed research as hand-written work would be misrepresentation and would collapse on

while keeping it from manufacturing results** — is both true and the more valuable 2026 skill. Fifty-six
