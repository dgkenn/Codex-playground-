# Niche tradable markets — ranked scan for a US small-bankroll retail trader (2026-06-15)

Consolidates ~17 market deep-dives (cascade of sub-agents) against the 7 profitable-traits rubric.
Every market scored 1-5 per trait; **trait 7 (+EV after friction) is the binding constraint nearly
everywhere.** Bottom line up front: **no niche market clears all seven as a clean, repeatable, mechanical
+EV edge for a small account.** The "least-dead" are CEF activist-coattail and (skill-gated) online poker.

## The pattern (identical to the project's meta-finding)
The two attractive traits — **soft recreational flow + a computable reference** — cluster in markets where
**friction (10-25% takeout/rake/vig/spread) or no-convergence** destroys the edge (collectibles, horse
racing, PredictIt, DFS, CEF). The **low-friction** markets are **HFT/AP/shark-dominated** (ETF NAV, DEX-CEX,
options, DFS). Wherever a market is *both* soft and low-friction in structure, it is **access-killed**
(sports exchanges have no surviving US venue). **Trait 7 is the wall**; no niche clears it mechanically.

## The "least-dead" three (if you insist on a niche)
1. **CEF activist-coattail** — buy deeply-discounted CEFs already targeted by Saba/Bulldog (13D filings are
   public); tenders/open-endings historically halve the discount. Real but lumpy, timing-uncertain,
   pro-rated, spread-eaten in the thin names. Event-driven speculation, not an arbitrage.
2. **Online poker** (NJ/PA/MI/NV/WV/DE) — a genuine, repeatable edge for a *skilled, studied* player who
   clears the ~5.5% rake (rakeback helps) and out-plays solver-armed regulars. It is a part-time JOB with
   variance, not a market you passively trade. Highest "skill ceiling" payoff of the set.
3. **Preferred/baby-bond discipline** — not alpha, but a high-yield income sleeve where a careful investor
   *avoids* the call-risk/negative-yield-to-call mistakes the retail crowd makes (always buy on
   yield-to-worst, below par, 3+ yrs to call). "Don't be the patsy," modestly.

## VERDICT
For a US small bankroll, **no non-Kalshi/non-Polymarket niche market offers a clean, repeatable,
mechanical +EV-after-friction edge.** This independently re-confirms the project's core conclusion: the
deployable answer is a **systematic risk-premium portfolio** (the trend-overlaid all-weather book +
contributions; see START_HERE.md / PROJECT_VERDICT.md), not a niche-market exploit. The only watch-item
with genuine future upside is a **CFTC-regulated sports/event EXCHANGE relaunch** (Sporttrade/Novig DCM) —
it would be ban-proof, nationwide, real-money, and recreational-flow; run the Pinnacle-CLV test then.
