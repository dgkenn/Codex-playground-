## The trilemma, stated plainly

**You cannot have all three of: 100% passive, $4,000/month, and little capital. Pick two.**

- passive + $4,000/mo → **needs $400K–1.1M**
- passive + little capital → yields roughly **$50–400/month**
- $4,000/mo + little capital → **requires labour**

That is arithmetic, not pessimism. And it is precisely why the internet is saturated with
passive-income courses: the real answer is "own $500,000," which nobody can sell you.
