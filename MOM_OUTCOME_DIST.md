# MOM_OUTCOME_DIST — Forward outcome distribution of the locked long-only crypto momentum sleeve

**Question this answers:** not "is there edge" (that's locked) but **"where do I likely end up, how
wide is the range, and what do I have to endure to get there?"** — so a US small-bankroll operator can
make an honest deploy/size decision.

**Locked config (NOT re-optimized)** — from `MOM_LONGONLY.md` (78d934e) + `MOM_LO_RISK.md` (03d714c):
top-15 deep USD-spot universe (Coinbase/Kraken), risk-adjusted 10d momentum (ret/vol), hold **top-3
equal-weight** (rest USDC cash), weekly rebalance, partial-rebalance 0.7, **BTC ≥ 100d-MA gate → cash**,
40 bps round-trip taker fee, **statically scaled c = 0.57 → ~40% annualized target vol**.

Strategy weekly net return series regenerated by reusing `mlo_backtest.py` / `mlo_risk.py`
(`backtest_risk(... k=3, top_n=15, gate_ma=100, partial=0.7, cost_bps=40) × 0.57`). Reproduces the
locked point estimates exactly: **FULL Sharpe +1.12, ann ret +45%, ann vol 40%, maxDD −45%**;
REC12 Sharpe +0.70 / maxDD −16% (matches the locked spec).

## METHOD (read this before the numbers)

- **Source series:** 386 weekly net returns, 2019-01-22 → 2026-06-09 (~7.4 yr, one full crypto cycle
  incl. 2021 mania, 2022 bear, 2023–24 recovery, 2025–26). Weekly mean 0.86%, sd 5.52%,
  **skew +1.50, excess kurtosis +7.4** (fat-tailed, right-skewed), worst week −17.7%, best +37.7%.
- **Why block bootstrap (primary method):** lag-1 autocorr of returns = 0.13, lag-1 autocorr of
  **|returns| = 0.39** → strong **vol-clustering**. An iid-normal/iid-t draw would *understate*
  drawdowns and underwater duration. We use a **moving-block bootstrap, block = 13 weeks (~1 quarter)**,
  20,000 paths per horizon, which preserves the autocorrelation and vol-clustering structure.
- **Parametric Monte-Carlo cross-check:** iid Student-t, df pinned from empirical kurtosis (≈4.8),
  moment-matched to mean/sd. It deliberately *drops* vol-clustering — used only to confirm the
  block-bootstrap terminal/DD numbers are in the same ballpark (they are; iid slightly *under*states
  DD as expected).
- **RAW vs HAIRCUT mean.** The in-sample mean is optimistic: the backtest universe is **survivorship-biased**
  (Coinbase/Kraken list only surviving pairs; dead alts are absent → upward bias) and crypto momentum may
  **structurally decay** as it gets crowded/arbitraged. We therefore **haircut the weekly mean by 35%**
  (ann ret 45% → 29%, Sharpe 1.12 → 0.73) and report **both**. Volatility/tail shape are kept at the
  historical (full-fat) level — we discount the *return*, not the *risk*. **Treat HAIRCUT as the base case.**

### CAVEATS (no false precision)
- **Past distribution ≠ future.** The bootstrap resamples 2019–2026 blocks; it cannot produce a regime
  the data never contained. If crypto enters a multi-year structural bear worse than 2022, or momentum
  decays toward zero edge, realized outcomes will be **below even the haircut tails**.
- The 35% haircut is a judgement, not a measurement. If decay is worse, shift toward the haircut p5–p25.
- Numbers are **ranges**, rounded. Do not read a "p50 = 1.41×" as a forecast — read the whole spread.

## 2. DRAWDOWN DISTRIBUTION — "what I endure along the way"

Peak-to-trough drawdown experienced *within* each horizon (block bootstrap). **Base = HAIRCUT.**

### P(experiencing a max drawdown worse than X)

| Horizon | >20% | >30% | >40% | >50% | E[max DD] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1yr (haircut) | 78% | 40% | 13% | 2% | −28% |
| **2yr (haircut)** | **97%** | **74%** | **41%** | **17%** | **−38%** |
| 3yr (haircut) | 100% | 89% | 60% | 32% | −45% |
| 1yr (raw) | 60% | 20% | 4% | 0% | −23% |
| 2yr (raw) | 87% | 49% | 18% | 5% | −31% |
| 3yr (raw) | 96% | 66% | 31% | 10% | −36% |

Max-drawdown percentiles (haircut, 2yr): p5 (best) −16% → median −37% → **p95 (worst) −60%**;
3yr p95 worst = **−67%**.

**Read:** a **>30% drawdown is the norm, not the exception** — base case ~74% over 2yr, ~89% over 3yr.
A >40% drawdown happens on ~40% of 2yr paths. You will almost certainly watch the sleeve cut roughly
in half at some point in a multi-year hold. The 100dMA gate caps tail DD vs un-gated long-only but does
**not** prevent deep drawdowns.

### Time underwater / time-to-recovery — "the thing that makes people quit"

Longest *continuous* stretch below a prior equity peak (weeks), block bootstrap, **HAIRCUT**:

| Horizon | p5 | p25 | median | p75 | p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1yr | 12w | 20w | **29w** | 42w | 51w |
| 2yr | 23w | 37w | **54w (~1yr)** | 79w | 102w (~2yr) |
| 3yr | 32w | 53w | **76w (~1.5yr)** | 111w | 153w (~3yr) |

Fraction of total time spent underwater (haircut): **median ~83–89%** across horizons.

**Read:** this is the brutal part. The **median longest underwater stretch is ~1 year over a 2yr hold,
~1.5 years over a 3yr hold**, and the sleeve sits below a prior high the *large majority of the time*
even on winning paths (because it grinds up through new highs). A multi-year underwater grind is a
**likely** experience, not a tail — and it is what causes operators to capitulate at the worst moment.

## 3. P(LOSS) / P(below cash) / P(below BTC)

Block bootstrap. P(below starting capital) ≡ P(below cash) since USDC cash earns ~0 here. P(below BTC)
is paired against a block-bootstrapped BTC buy-and-hold over the same horizon.

| Horizon | P(below start / cash) HAIRCUT | RAW | P(below BTC buy-hold) HAIRCUT | RAW |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1yr | **39%** | 25% | 60% | 52% |
| 2yr | **31%** | 16% | 62% | 53% |
| 3yr | **26%** | 10% | 66% | 54% |

**Read:**
- **Down-money risk is real and front-loaded:** base case ~39% chance you're underwater at 1yr,
  ~31% at 2yr. It *falls* with horizon (drift compounds), but never disappears.
- **It loses to BTC buy-and-hold more often than not (~60–66% of paths, haircut).** That is the key
  comparison for a small operator: simple BTC hold has higher historical drift and beats this sleeve on
  the majority of paths. The sleeve's case is **lower drawdown-to-cash via the gate and diversification
  across top-3 momentum names**, and a fatter *right* tail in alt-led regimes — not beating BTC on the
  median. (Note the BTC benchmark is itself survivorship-clean only for BTC; alts in the sleeve carry
  the upward universe bias the haircut is meant to offset.)

## 4. SIZING IMPLICATION — turning "size it small" into a number

Anchor: the **worst-5% (1-in-20) two-year peak-to-trough drawdown under the haircut mean ≈ −60%**
(worst-1% ≈ −68%). Sizing rule: choose sleeve fraction **f of total net worth** so that the dollar
loss on a 1-in-20 bad path is one you can stomach without forced selling:

> **f ≤ (tolerable % of net worth you can lose) / 0.60**

| Tolerable NW loss on a 1-in-20 path | Max sleeve size (% of net worth) |
|---|---|
| 2% | **~3%** |
| 3% | **~5%** |
| 5% | **~8%** |
| 7.5% | ~13% |
| 10% | ~17% |

**Concrete guideline:** for a typical small-bankroll operator who wants a bad-luck 2yr stretch to cost
**no more than ~3–5% of total net worth**, hold this sleeve at **~5% of net worth, and not more than
~10%** even with high risk tolerance. At 5% of NW, the 1-in-20 path is a ~3% NW hit and the *median*
drawdown experience (~−37%) is a ~1.8% NW dip — survivable, so you don't capitulate mid-grind. Sizing it
larger turns a normal −40%+ drawdown into a net-worth event that forces a sale at the bottom, which is
exactly how positive-EV bets get converted into realized losses.

---

## 5. HONEST TWO-SIDED FRAMING

**This is a high-variance, positive-expected-value bet. Both sides, plainly:**

**Upside (real):** even after the 35% haircut, the median 2yr outcome is +40% and the upper quartile
roughly doubles; the top 5% of 2/3yr paths are 5×–9×. The positive drift and fat right tail are genuine —
in an alt-led up-regime this sleeve compounds fast, and the 100dMA gate historically pulled it to cash
before the worst of the bear, capping cash-relative losses better than naive long alt exposure.

**Downside (equally real):** base case ~31% chance you're below your starting money at 2yr; it
**underperforms simply holding BTC on ~60–66% of paths**; a **>30% drawdown is ~74% likely over 2yr**,
and the **median longest underwater stretch is ~1 year** (with the equity below a prior high most of the
time). The 1-in-20 path roughly halves your capital and stays painful for a long time.

**Net:** deploy only as a **small satellite sleeve (~5%, ≤10% of net worth)**, with eyes open that the
expected path includes a long underwater grind and a meaningful chance of trailing both cash and BTC.
The edge is positive but the variance and decay risk are large; **position size — not the signal — is
what makes this honest to hold.** And remember: the bootstrap can only replay history's distribution;
genuine structural decay or a worse-than-2022 regime would land you below even these haircut tails.
