Before you go

  • Keep this page open and the screen on. iOS suspends background tabs; the coaching stops if you switch away or the screen locks. The page requests a wake lock, but keep it foreground.
  • Silent switch off. Web audio respects it on some iOS versions.
  • Falling pair = ease off. Rising pair = lift. Single note = back on pace. Silence = you are inside the band.
