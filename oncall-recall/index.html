Anesthesia Tier 1 Critical care Car-safe

What two gastric parameters define the threshold at which a patient is at high risk for aspiration pneumonitis?

Answer
Gastric volume >25 mL (0.4 mL/kg) and gastric pH <2.5
Why
Large volumes of acidic gastric contents cause severe chemical pneumonitis (Mendelson syndrome) when aspirated.
Source — Morgan & Mikhail, p. 438  ·  topic: Aspiration: prevention and management
