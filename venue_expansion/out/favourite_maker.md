## 3. EV (fee-free, cents) -- SIGN ONLY, per QUOTED contract beside per FILLED contract

Per-quoted EV = sum of P&L over filled quotes / ALL quotes (unfilled = 0). SE and t are day-clustered (CR1, equal weight, df = days - 1) and are **for information only: this spec has no EV t-gate.** Reason, frozen in advance: the cluster unit is the calendar day, the window holds 60 days, so the day count is saturated and cannot be raised by adding hours; the favourite-side per-contract sd is about 36c (worse than the longshot side's 22c); a Bonferroni t-bar near 3.2 (24 comparisons) would need EV of roughly +5 to +10c per quoted contract against an expected ~1c per fill. The t **cannot** reach the bar here and the verdict does not ask it to. The gate that decides is the proportion test of section 1 (99.79% Wilson). Per-filled EV = 100 x P(YES|filled) - mean(d) exactly.
